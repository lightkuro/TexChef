What it does

  1. 01

    Type LaTeX. Watch the PDF.

    A full TeX Live runs in your browser. The PDF follows a moment after you stop typing.

  2. 02

    Click the PDF. Type the change.

    Click a sentence, change a number, press Enter. TexChef edits the source for you.

  3. 03

    Write without the backslashes.

    Headings, bold text and lists show formatted as you write. Same LaTeX underneath.

  4. 04

    One master. Many tailored versions.

    Make a version per job or journal. Fix something once and every version can take the fix.

  5. 05

    Two documents, side by side.

    Any two documents or versions, with the differences marked. Copy a line or a block across.

  6. 06

    Yours, on your device.

    Documents and every version live in your browser. Connect Google Drive for a backup and your other devices.

Start with a blank article, a template, or your own .tex files.
